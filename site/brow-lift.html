Questions

Frequently asked questions.

A brow lift, or forehead lift, raises eyebrows that have dropped and settled low with age, restoring them to a more natural, youthful height. Lifting the brow opens up the eyes, softens a heavy or frowning look and refreshes the whole upper face. Hagen offers several techniques, from a precise direct brow lift to a fuller forehead lift, and recommends the one that suits your brows and your goals.

A direct brow lift is Hagen's most requested technique. Through a fine incision hidden along the upper border of the eyebrow, the brow is lifted and secured in a natural position. It is more targeted than an endoscopic or full forehead lift, can often be done under local anaesthetic as a day case, has less downtime and is especially good for lifting heavy or uneven brows. The scar sits in the brow line and typically fades to be barely noticeable.

Forehead reduction, also called hairline lowering, brings a high or long hairline forward to shorten the forehead and bring the face into more balanced proportion. Where appropriate it can be combined with a brow lift. Hagen will assess your hairline, forehead height and brow position together and advise whether it would suit you.

A direct brow lift can often be performed under local anaesthetic as a comfortable day case, so you are awake but relaxed and go home the same day. Larger forehead lifts or combined procedures may be carried out under general anaesthetic. Hagen will recommend the most appropriate and comfortable option for you.

Expect some bruising and swelling around the brows and forehead that settles over about one to two weeks, and cold compresses help. Stitches are dissolvable. Most people feel comfortable returning to work and social life after around two weeks, and many find the bruising fades within the first week.

With a direct brow lift the fine incision sits along the upper edge of the eyebrow, where it is camouflaged by the brow hair and fades well; other techniques place incisions within the hairline. You will receive personalised scar-care guidance, and in our patients' experience the scar line is often neater than expected.

Yes, and it often is. Heavy brows and hooded upper lids frequently go together, so a brow lift and an upper blepharoplasty can complement each other beautifully. Hagen will assess your brows and eyelids as a whole and, if it suits you, plan your rejuvenation in one coordinated step rather than treating each area in isolation.
